# Nitheesh S P — Haute Fashion Technology & Concept Design Portfolio

An editorial luxury fashion portfolio website engineered for **Nitheesh S P** — Fashion Technology Student & Concept Designer (B.Tech Fashion Technology, Jai Shriram Engineering College).

---

## 🏛️ Project Overview & Design Philosophy

Inspired by luxury fashion editorials, contemporary European haute couture ateliers, and runway monographs:
- **Designer Identity:** Nitheesh S P (Fashion Technology · Class of 2027)
- **Creative Focus:** Streetwear, biological adaptations, camouflage systems, warning coloration, feline velocity mechanics, and contemporary youth culture.
- **Architectural Aesthetic:** Warm ivory (`#F9F7F2`), charcoal obsidian (`#121211`), muted olive (`#4D563E`), and earth brown accents with dual **Atelier Light** and **Nocturne Dark** themes.
- **Master Boards:** Curated landscape boards extracted directly from the designer's 5 official collection PDFs.

---

## 📁 5 Curated Collections Included

1. **01. Chroma Nature (SS 27)** — *Frog Theme / Poison Dart Frog ("Beautifully Dangerous")*
   - Concept: Toxic warning coloration (aposematism) translated into explosive streetwear.
   - Boards: Storyboard, Moodboard, Colour Board (Pantone 19-4517 TCX, 12-0825 TCX, 19-0303 TCX), Graphic/Print Board, Streetwear Jacket Flats (front & back), Cargo Pants Flats (front & back), Final Look Board.
2. **02. Camo Leaf (AW 27)** — *Featured Collection*
   - Concept: Leaves, forest textures, and organic camouflage exploring the dialogue between concealment and revelation.
   - Boards: Storyboard, Moodboard, Colour/Print Board (Pantone 18-0228 TCX, 15-1119 TCX, 19-1118 TCX, 19-4001 TCX), Utility Jacket Flats (front & back), Matching Trousers Flats (front & back), Final Look Board.
3. **03. Wild Instinct (AW 27)** — *Cheetah / Feline Velocity*
   - Concept: Predatory speed, rosettes, and power dressing through tactile embroidery and 3D applique.
   - Boards: Storyboard, Moodboard, Colour/Print/Embroidery/Applique Board (Pantone 19-3911 TCX, 16-1439 TCX, 12-0817 TCX, 18-1659 TCX), Outerwear Flats (front & back), Tailored Trousers Flats (front & back), Final Look Board.
4. **04. Strie (AW 27)** — *Zebra Striations & Optical Rhythm*
   - Concept: Optical zebra striations treated as architectural music; monochrome contrast with High Risk Red punctuation.
   - Boards: Storyboard, Moodboard, Colour/Print Board (Pantone 18-1764 TCX, 16-4702 TCX, 19-3911 TCX, 14-4202 TCX, 17-3914 TCX), Striated Jacket Flats (front & back), Engineered Trousers Flats (front & back), Final Look Board.
5. **05. Linear Elegance (SS 27)** — *Subverted Pinstripes & Floral Embroidery*
   - Concept: Recontextualizing heritage pinstripes by contrasting rigid menswear tailoring with delicate floral embroidery and relaxed knitwear.
   - Boards: Storyboard, Moodboard, Colour/Print/Embroidery Board (Pantone 11-2509 TCX, 13-2808 TCX, 19-1664 TCX), Deconstructed Blazer Flats (front & back), Wide-Leg Trousers Flats (front & back), Final Look Board.

## ⚡ Technical Stack

- **Framework:** React 19 + Vite 6
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS 3 (Luxury custom typography, bespoke palette, noise grain texture)
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Typography:** *Cormorant Garamond*, *Italiana*, *Inter*, *JetBrains Mono*
- **Deployment Ready:** Netlify configuration with `netlify.toml`

---

## 🚀 Deployment to Netlify

This repository is pre-configured for automated Netlify deployment:

1. Import this repository in [Netlify](https://app.netlify.com).
2. The build settings are auto-detected from `netlify.toml`:
   - **Build Command:** `npm run build`
   - **Publish Directory:** `dist`
3. Click **Deploy Site**.
